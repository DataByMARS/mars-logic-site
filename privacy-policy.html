Privacy Policy

Privacy, stated plainly.

Effective August 5, 2026

Overview

This website provides information about MARS—Modern Analytics & Research Solutions. It is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages.

Information you choose to send

The contact and newsletter forms prepare a message in your email application. The website itself does not store or process the information entered into those forms. Once you send an email, it is handled by your email provider and the MARS email service.

Hosting and technical logs

GitHub Pages may process standard technical information needed to deliver the site, such as IP address, browser information, requested pages, and timestamps. That processing is governed by GitHub’s own policies.

External resources

The site loads the Poppins typeface from Google Fonts and currently uses selected media hosted by Wix to reproduce the licensed template’s visual presentation. Those providers may receive basic request information when the resource loads.

Cookies and analytics

MARS does not currently set advertising cookies or run a first-party analytics service on this website. If analytics or additional form services are introduced, this policy should be updated before those services are activated.

External links

Links to external websites are governed by the privacy practices of those websites. MARS is not responsible for the content or practices of third-party sites.
