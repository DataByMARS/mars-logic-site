About

Evidence should be rigorous, clear, and built to be used.

MARS connects research and analytics into one disciplined process, designed around the decision the work must support.

The Story of MARS

One connected path from questions to answers.

MARS—Modern Analytics & Research Solutions—was established to reduce the fragmentation that often separates research design, data collection, analysis, visualization, and reporting.

That connection matters. Decisions made early in a project determine what can be measured later, which analyses will be defensible, and whether the final result can answer the original question.

MARS can support one phase or manage the full engagement, while keeping methods, assumptions, limitations, and deliverables aligned from beginning to end.

Working Principles

How the work is approached.

01

Start with the decision

Clarify what the work must support before choosing methods or measures.

02

Match method to question

Use the simplest defensible approach that can answer the actual research question.

03

Keep reasoning visible

Document assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, and analytical choices throughout.

04

Build for use

Design visuals and reporting for the audience that must understand and act.

Engagement Process

A disciplined project rhythm.

  1. 01

    Define

    Clarify the decision, audience, constraints, research questions, and success criteria.

  2. 02

    Design

    Select methods, measures, instruments, sampling, analysis, and deliverables.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Collect and manage evidence with clear quality controls and documentation.

  4. 04

    Analyze

    Apply appropriate quantitative and qualitative methods and review the reasoning.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Translate findings into clear visuals, reports, and decision-ready communication.
